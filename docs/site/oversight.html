Robustness checks

The exact-evidence order replay changed all 18 answer presentations. Mean rank agreement was Kendall τ=0.65; all three judges retained the same top candidate, but the two lower-capability judges each fell to τ=0.52. Presentation order is therefore a real uncertainty, especially below the strongest judge.

Across both panels, the adaptive probe improved 6 rankings, left 4 unchanged, and worsened 2. Pooled accuracy moved from 72.6% to 74.6%. The mechanism is operationally useful, but its effect is not uniformly positive.
