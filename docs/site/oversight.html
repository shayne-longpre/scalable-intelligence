Protocol health

357 model calls, including 1 paid failed run attempt, 1 zero-call setup failure, 4 structured-JSON repairs, 0 visible-text retries, and 2 recorded unavailable answers. Failed attempts are included in the $19.57 total.

Interpretation

The catalog score is an external reference, not ground truth. Pairs separated by less than two score points are reported separately.

The report deliberately keeps two questions separate: whether the judge ranks answers consistently, and whether the judge invented a valid test in the first place. A weak judge can create an underspecified or impossible problem and then confidently punish the candidate that notices. Those failures are substantive evidence about the oversight frontier, not formatting noise.
