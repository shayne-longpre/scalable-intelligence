Evaluation taxonomy

A compact coding frame for what evaluators do and what their questions test. It is a review aid, not a complete theory of intelligence or a validated psychometric instrument.

How to read it

Evaluation strategies describe how a model gathers or uses evidence. Question types describe the capability a probe is trying to elicit. A single probe may receive several labels.

Structural facts such as speaker, round, probe identity, target, and whether a follow-up was generated after seeing evidence come from transcript metadata. Semantic labels are conservative automated suggestions until audited by people.

Source relations Close match follows an established assessment family. Derived combines related literatures into a practical bucket. Observed captures behavior seen in these experiments.
